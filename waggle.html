method

Everything above is checkable

A recommendation engine nobody can inspect is indistinguishable from an advertisement for whichever venue it favours. So the weights, the definitions and the queries are public, and they change in public.

Surviving

Still meeting the liquidity and trade thresholds seven days after launch. One definition, frozen, versioned when it changes.

Adjusted volume

Raw volume minus flow matching wash patterns. Raw is always shown beside it so you can see what was removed.

Confidence

Every figure carries its sample size. Below the floor it reads low and is never quietly averaged into a score.

Coverage

A chain without a collector shows empty, not estimated. Gaps are stated rather than filled in.

What Waggle will never do

  • Predict that a launch will succeed. It describes structural fit, nothing more.
  • Take payment from a launchpad for placement or for a score.
  • Hold a position in any venue it scores.
  • Show a figure without its sample size and confidence.
  • Use a submitted project in any public output. Submissions stay private.
